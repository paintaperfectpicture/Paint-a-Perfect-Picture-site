2) Multi-Unit Turns & Property Maintenance Painting

Turn-ready unit repaints and repeatable maintenance programs aligned to leasing schedules. Built for consistency across units and common areas.

  • Unit turns, hallways, stairwells, common areas
  • Repeatable scope standards for predictable results
  • Fast resets aligned to access windows
  • Optional photo documentation for closeout

3) Residential Interior & Exterior Painting

Residential projects are welcomed with clean prep, protection, and premium finishes that enhance property value and presentation.

  • Interior walls, ceilings, trim, doors (as scoped)
  • Exterior siding/trim/doors with weather-appropriate prep
  • Clean jobsite standards: protection + daily cleanup
  • Color guidance available upon request

4) Drywall Repair & Paint-Ready Finishing

Repairs that disappear under paint. We patch, feather, sand, prime, and finish for uniform blending.

  • Patching holes, cracks, tape/mud, sanding, blend finishing
  • Texture match (scope dependent)
  • Prime-before-paint to prevent flashing
  • For perfect uniformity, repainting the full wall/ceiling is often best

5) Industrial Coatings & Fireproofing Support (Spec-Based)

Spec-based support for industrial coatings and fireproofing systems. Final system selection depends on rated assemblies, thickness requirements, substrate, and inspection criteria.

  • Firestopping support for penetrations/joints as required
  • SFRM / intumescent / cementitious support (spec dependent)
  • Industrial coating support: surface prep planning + durable systems (as specified)
  • Optional documentation support for inspection readiness and closeout
Note: Fireproofing and coating systems are executed per project specification. Site conditions, access constraints, and compliance requirements may impact final approach and pricing.
