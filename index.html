Industrial coatings support Corrosion protection and durable coatings (as specified) for industrial environments.
Fireproofing levels Multiple systems depending on spec—SFRM, intumescent, cementitious, and firestopping.
Fireproofing levels (multiple options)
  • Level 1 — Firestopping: Fire caulk/sealants, collars, putty pads, pillows for penetrations and rated assemblies.
  • Level 2 — SFRM: Spray-applied fire-resistive material for structural steel/decks where specified.
  • Level 3 — Intumescent: Thin-film coating that expands under heat (often used where appearance matters).
  • Level 4 — Cementitious: Durable fireproofing systems for industrial/structural protection.
  • Level 5 — Documentation support: Photo records and closeout support for inspection readiness (as requested).

Final system selection depends on building requirements/specs (rated assembly, thickness, substrate, and inspection criteria).
